LIS. Creative Studio — Graphic and Web Design Studio in Rosario, Argentina

LIS. Creative Studio is a design studio founded in 2025 by Ornela Cillo, a graphic designer with more than 10 years of experience, based in Rosario, Argentina. It works remotely with clients in Argentina, Spain, Uruguay and other countries, specializing in architecture, interior design and real estate studios and creative brands that need a consistent, professional visual presence.

Services

  • Visual identity — logo, palette, typography and brand system
  • Web design — custom websites, landing pages and digital experiences
